# Short-form style reference (style only, never copy content)
Distilled from ~58 analysed Reels/Shorts (docs/tervek/videok/ig-*.md). Use as inspiration: "inspired by, not a copy".

## Captions
- Word by word or 2-3 word chunks, 2-3 words/s, one line (two at most), lower third, bold sans, white, dark outline or pill.
- One accent colour for the key word only (orange/cyan/yellow). The spoken word lights up, the rest stays dim.
- Size about 56-76 px on 1080x1920; a chunk stays on screen >= 0.25 s (QA minimum). Keep faces out of the caption zone.
- Hierarchy: quiet word captions all the way, a big title card only on the 1-2 strongest lines.

## Tempo and cuts
- A new visual element every 2-4 s (a cut, zoom, card or b-roll); never one static shot for more than ~12 s.
- Every cut follows the sentence: the picture names what the voice says. Hard cut or punch-in on the emphasised word.
- Rhythm breaker at the key beat (face close-up, number card, silence) instead of constant motion.
- No source-cut flashes inside a candidate; no filler tail, end right at the peak.

## Hook (first 2 s)
- The first line is a complete, relatable claim, number or tension: "He paid $1M for a shelf", not a description.
- On-screen hook 3-7 words, same promise as the title and the first spoken line; the payoff comes at the end.
- A number plus a promise ("42 things", "$15,000 all-in") beats a topic label. No intro, no greeting.

## Structure
- Progress cue for list/lesson content (step counter, 01/03). One accent palette, no more than 2 fonts.
- Close on the payoff line or a callback to the opening line (invites a rewatch). CTA only when the campaign needs it.
